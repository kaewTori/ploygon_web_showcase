ABOUT PROJECT

No Tales in the Sea is a Web-interactive and Digital art project that uses endangered species to create Low-Polygon Art, combining creativity and conservation to present 20 endangered species from around the world, each represented by 20 carefully crafted geometric pieces that create unique visual patterns. The project not only showcases the beauty and fragility of these animals, but also raises awareness about their conservation by combining design, storytelling, and environmental advocacy into a powerful experience.
